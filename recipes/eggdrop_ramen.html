Eggdrop Ramen

Description

A very simple recipe for eggdrop ramen. My favorite flavor combination is one package each of "Creamy Chicken" and "Chili" flavors.

It is an excellent way to add some nutritious protein to your sodium saturated, empty carbohydrate filled ramen noodles!

Ingredients

  • 2 packages of Instant Ramen Noodles (Any flavor)
  • 3 Whole Eggs

Steps

  1. Bring 4 Cups water to full boil in a pot.
  2. Add Ramen Noodles. Cook for 2 minutes.
  3. Crack open eggs and add to boiling noodles.
  4. Cook for 5 minutes, stirring gently.
  5. Remove from heat. Gently stir in seasoning packets.
  6. Enjoy!
