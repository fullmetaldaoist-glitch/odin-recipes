Big Batch Biscuits and Gravy

Description

My above average homemade biscuits and gravy. Made with zest and spice!

This gravy recipe is 4 times as large as most. Extra gravy can be refrigerated for up to a week. It can also be frozen.

Biscuits are better when made fresh.

Ingredients

    Gravy

  • 2 Pounds Ground Spicy Italian Sausage
  • 1 Pound Ground Sage Breakfast Sausage
  • 1 Pound Ground Beef
  • 1 Full Stick REAL Butter
  • 2 Cups White Unbleached Flour
  • 1 Gallon Whole Milk
  • Salt and Pepper to taste

    • Biscuits

  • 2 Cups Bisquick Baking Mix
  • 2/3 Cup Whole Milk
  • 2 Tbs Vegetable Oil

Steps

    Gravy

  1. Brown all ground meats in large pot on medium heat. Do not drain.
  2. Melt butter into browned meat.
  3. Add flour until all grease is absorbed. At least 1/2 cup to 2 cups at most.
  4. Stir continuously until flour begins to get toasty.
  5. Slowly stir in one gallon of milk.
  6. Keep stirring to prevent scorching the bottom, until it starts to boil.
  7. Remove from heat and let sit, adding salt and pepper to taste.
  8. Gravy will thicken as it cools.

    Biscuits

  1. Prehead oven to 425 degrees.
  2. Cover baking sheet with parchment paper.
  3. Mix Bisquick, Milk, and Vegetable Oil together in large bowl.
  4. Drop dough by spoonfulls onto baking sheet.
  5. Bake for 12 to 14 minutes, until golder brown.

    Serving

  1. Take 2 biscuits, split in half, and put on plate.
  2. Smother completely in gravy.
  3. Enjoy!
